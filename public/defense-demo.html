Actions

2
Enter actual volume collected: e.g. 120 kg (optional but encouraged)
3

Status lifecycle
PLANNED → IN_PROGRESS → COMPLETED is automatic. The first pickup transitions to IN_PROGRESS; the last pickup transitions to COMPLETED. No manual status change required — no human error possible.
